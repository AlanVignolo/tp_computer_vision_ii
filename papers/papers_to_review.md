1. [Recent advances in crack detection technologies for structures](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/built-environment/articles/10.3389/fbuil.2024.1321634/full) — Kaveh & Alhajj, 2024, Frontiers in Built Environment. Provides a comprehensive map of 85 crack detection papers from 2022-2023, cataloging datasets, algorithms, and metrics so you can ground your Introduction and justify architecture choices.
2. [Accuracy–Efficiency Trade-Off: Optimizing YOLOv8 for Structural Crack Detection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252445/) — Zhang et al., 2025. Demonstrates that an optimized YOLOv8 achieves 88.7% mAP@0.5 with 16% fewer parameters, directly informing your Phase 1 choice of YOLO architecture and potential optimization strategies.
3. [Pavement-DETR: A High-Precision Real-Time Detection Transformer for Pavement Defect Detection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12031190/) — Zuo et al., 2025, Sensors. Shows that transformer-based detectors (RT-DETR) can match or exceed YOLO on pavement defects, giving you a viable second architecture to compare against YOLO in Phase 1.
4. [Concrete Road Crack Detection Using Deep Learning-Based Faster R-CNN Method](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40996-021-00671-2) — Hacıefendioğlu & Başağa, 2022. Uniquely tests crack detection under varying weather and lighting conditions, giving you the Faster R-CNN perspective for your architecture comparison and evidence that robustness matters for real deployment.
5. [Pavement Fatigue Crack Detection and Severity Classification Based on Convolutional Neural Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16021) — Wang et al., 2024. The closest paper to your combined approach: uses a CNN for both crack detection AND severity classification using the DIM standard, achieving 96.7% severity accuracy.
6. [DeepCrack: A Deep Hierarchical Feature Learning Architecture for Crack Segmentation](https://yhlleo.github.io/papers/DeepCrack-Neurocomputing2019.pdf) — Liu et al., 2019, Neurocomputing. The foundational paper for the segmentation dataset you already have, providing benchmark results and architectural context you'll need when training your Phase 2 segmentation model.
7. [A crack detection and quantification method using matched filter and photograph reconstruction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-08280-z) — Liu et al., 2025, Nature Scientific Reports. Directly addresses the scale problem by converting pixel measurements to real-world crack dimensions using UAV camera parameters with <2% error, offering a potential methodology for severity quantification.
8. [Automated classification and severity assessment of alligator cracks and raveling in asphalt pavement using ResNet50](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/oa-edit/10.1201/9781003595120-199/automated-classification-severity-assessment-alligator-cracks-raveling-asphalt-pavement-using-resnet50-ullah-amir-henry) — Ullah et al., 2025. Uses ResNet50 for alligator crack severity classification, a direct precedent for your severity classification approach.
9. [Topology-Preserving Deep Image Segmentation](http://papers.neurips.cc/paper/8803-topology-preserving-deep-image-segmentation.pdf) — Hu et al., 2019, NeurIPS. Introduces a topology-aware loss function that penalizes broken connections in thin structures, directly applicable to keeping crack branches connected during segmentation on DeepCrack.
10. [Enhanced retinal blood vessels segmentation using deep learning with U-Net architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666521225000675) — Lamrani et al., 2025, Biomedical Engineering Advances. Standard U-Net applied to vessel segmentation, a good baseline reference since cracks and retinal vessels share similar thin, branching morphology.
11. [Topology-Aware Loss for Aorta and Great Vessel Segmentation in CT Images](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.03137v2) — Ozcelik et al., 2023, arXiv / Medical Image Analysis. Practical implementation of topology-aware loss you could adapt to keep crack segments connected during training.
12. [Retinal Vessel Segmentation using variants of UNET (GitHub)](https://github.com/arkanivasarkar/Retinal-Vessel-Segmentation-using-variants-of-UNET) — implementations of multiple U-Net variants that can be directly adapted to crack segmentation.
13. [A Review of Deep Learning-Based Methods for Road Extraction from Remote Sensing Images](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/16/12/2056) — 2024, Remote Sensing. Comprehensive review of connectivity-preserving techniques for thin linear features, transferable to crack branching preservation.
